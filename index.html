    Profit = what the item sells for (lowest price on this server times how many one craft makes, minus the 5% auction house cut) minus the cost of its materials. Each material uses its lowest price on this server, or the vendor price when a vendor sells it for gold and that is cheaper. Green is profit, red is a loss. In MoP Classic most raw materials (ore, bars, cloth, herbs, leather, elementals) trade on the region-wide market, which Blizzard's API currently returns empty, so many recipes cannot be fully priced.     Items vendors sell for this currency alone, ranked by gold you get back per 1,000 spent. Each item is worth the better of two ways out: selling it to any vendor (its vendor sell price), or listing it on this server's auction house at the lowest price minus the 5% cut, which is only possible when it doesn't bind on pickup. Most PvP gear binds on pickup, so its value is the vendor price. Costs come from Blizzard's item cost table (via wago.tools) matched to vendor lists from the MaNGOS Four database, so a few items may no longer be sold in MoP Classic.

    Crafting professions

    Gathering professions

    Crafting professions are ranked by what their crafted items sell for on this server: estimated gold sold per day once sales tracking has a few hours of data, otherwise the value of what is listed now at the lowest price. Items with only one listing are left out of the listed value, since single listings are often priced unrealistically. Material costs are not taken off, because MoP Classic crafting materials sell on the region-wide market, which Blizzard's API currently returns empty. Tap a profession to see its top items.
