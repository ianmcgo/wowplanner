    Crafting professions

    Gathering professions

    Crafting professions are ranked by what their crafted items sell for on this server: estimated gold sold per day once sales tracking has a few hours of data, otherwise the value of what is listed now at the lowest price. Items with only one listing are left out of the listed value, since single listings are often priced unrealistically. Material costs are not taken off, because MoP Classic crafting materials sell on the region-wide market, which Blizzard's API currently returns empty. Tap a profession to see its top items.
